Select steps with the mouse on Legend Cube's canvas and put them in a container, as in Alteryx: Shift and a drag draws a box that adds the steps inside it, Shift and a click selects a step or deselects it, and Escape or a click on the canvas clears the selection. A selected step's menu has Put Selection in a Container, as Ctrl/Cmd+G does: the container takes the steps selected and each step between them, so it stays one block of linked steps. The selection is the page's, never saved in the cube.

Notes beside the steps on Legend Cube's canvas: one plain-text note per step, written in the Note field of its editor or from its menu (Add Note…), and shown as a muted line under it, with the whole note on hover. Notes are saved in the spec's `meta.notes`. They follow their step when it is copied, pasted, duplicated or removed, and only change how the canvas shows the cube, never what it runs.

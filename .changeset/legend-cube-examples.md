Legend Cube ships sample data and example cubes: Sports and Trades sample models beside Northwind in the Model tab (made-up, seeded rows in an in-memory H2 database), and an Examples tab in the source dialog that opens one of six example cubes over them in place of the cube, as one undo step, and runs it. The empty canvas offers "open an example".

Give element type letters and icons (`.color--*`) a shade per theme that keeps their hue and reaches 3:1 on panels, headers and the selected row. In the light theme, enumeration, measure, unit, association, profile, dependency, mapping, function, service, flat-data, database and connection use darker shades. In the dark theme, the class, schema and SingleStore function icons use lighter shades; their `.background--*` fills are unchanged.

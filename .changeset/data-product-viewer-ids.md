The data product viewer shows IDs next to the titles, and one click copies an ID: under the title, the deployed data product ID and the deployment ID (DID); after each access point group and access point title, its ID. An ID that the title already shows is not repeated, and the header shows no IDs without a deployment (as in Studio's preview). A description that only repeats its title (ignoring case and spacing) is no longer shown, for the data product, its access point groups and its access points.
